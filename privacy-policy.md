# Privacy Policy for CollageMaker

_Last updated: September 16, 2026_

CollageMaker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## Photo Library Access
CollageMaker requests access to your photo library solely so you can:
- Select photos to place into a collage layout
- Save the finished collage back to your photo library

All photo selection, editing, and processing happens entirely on your device. No photo, image data, or metadata is ever uploaded, transmitted over a network, or shared with the developer or any third party.

## Data Collection
CollageMaker collects no analytics, usage data, advertising identifiers, or any other information. The app has no user accounts, no network requests, and no third-party SDKs.

## Children's Privacy
CollageMaker does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "last updated" date.
